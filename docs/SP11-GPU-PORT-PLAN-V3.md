# SP11 GPU port plan, version 3: A16 (Adreno X2-90, a8xx) to SP11 (Adreno X1-85, a7xx, gen70500)

Goal: stock macOS 27 (26A428) WindowServer composites through Metal on the Surface Pro 11. Written 2026-11-22 as version 3 of [SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN.md](SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN.md) ("v1": the inventory of the kext and the plug-in, the work items W1 to W24 and the first-contact safety plan) and as an extension of [SP11-GPU-A7XX-STUDY.md](SP11-GPU-A7XX-STUDY.md) ("the study"). It applies every correction and addition of an independent fact-check of the previous draft (verdict SOUND WITH CORRECTIONS; a host-only note that is not in this repository; section 0 lists its items), keeps v1's ids (K, P, W), and replaces v1's stage ladder S0 to S7 for gates X8a and X8b by the stages E1 to E7 of [SP11-GPU-BRINGUP-STAGES.md](SP11-GPU-BRINGUP-STAGES.md) (the "stages file"), which also holds the long data lists (the `hw_init` order, the GMU image layout, the restore rules, the pinned inputs and the checks).

Labels. VERIFIED: read in the cited pinned public text, or shown by a host test that ran; never a statement about the unit. OBSERVED: seen on the unit in boots 29 to 31 (one unit, one boot each) or declared in the unit's own firmware tables, read offline (the firmware's declaration, not a read of the hardware; stated as such where used). INFERRED, EXPERIMENTAL, UNIMPLEMENTED and UNKNOWN as in the study. Effort: S under a day, M a few days, L a week or more.

## 1. Verdict

1. The stack splits cleanly. **Generic** (the guest kernel is the same J700/T8140 26A428, so the IOGPU and IOMFB class layouts, PAC and the kext-to-monitor HVC ABI do not change): the kext's IOGPU logic, resource lists, fences, recovery policy, the Metal object model, staging, shader-cache glue and the AIR front end. **Silicon-specific** (all of the work): power, clocks, the GMU, its address space, the ring and the CP start at EL2; the a7xx register set and descriptors in the plug-in; the firewall tables; a few compiler ABI items.
2. **The critical path is hardware-gated bring-up, not code.** Boot 30: CX off, GX not up, no GMU running, nothing behind CX readable (OBSERVED: the words; INFERRED: the verdict). Nothing of S1 to S6 has touched the unit; the unit has no host link, UART or log (a payload's page is read from a film). Plan on **7 to 9 supervised boots** (E1, E2, E3a, E3b, E3c and E4 to E7; 7 only if E1 and E2, and E3a and E3b, are each chained) before any guest is involved, each possibly repeated (an estimate, INFERRED).
3. New against v1: (a) the bring-up must be **freestanding C at EL2** (the q1n1 fork's X8a model, `tools/sp11_gpu_x8a.py` with its fake device, is Python over a link that the SP11 lacks) (3.1, N1); (b) **the payload runner masks SErrors and exposes no poll to payloads**, so a bad GPU read can go unnoticed while the payload carries on and writes (8.4, N2); (c) the X8a model lacks items that Linux needs: the PLL1 and GCC GPLL0 votes, the DDR bandwidth vote and the ACD message (the host-side votes that Linux issues before the GMU boots; it votes no CX or MX rail), the `qcom_scm` aperture, the a7xx power-up register list and the full `hw_init`, and the 4-word GBIF CX table (public: `0x00071620` x 4) (3.1); (d) gen70500 passes the compute driver parameters in the constant file, not a preamble UBO: **BINDING-ABI v7** (3.4); (e) the GMEM and CCU port (W12) shrinks to one register (3.3); (f) the Linux DT and the catalog say the GPU is coherent and the unit's IORT says non-coherent (OBSERVED once, the table): the same DT-versus-IORT dispute that the A16 had, where a PMU self-test decides (3.2); (g) the GPU thermal sensors are tsens3 hardware sensors 5 to 12 (3.1, 8.3); (h) the absence of the KDK blocks less than feared: the private headers are **unchanged** for the SP11 (INFERRED: the kernelcache is the same) (7); (i) `gen70500_gmu.bin` and `gen70500_sqe.fw` are **staged** locally (7); (j) **major: 80,532 of the GMU image's 81,312 bytes load at GMU IOVA `0x4000`, so the GMU's own SMMU mapping (stream 5), a DRAM arena and its page tables are mandatory in Linux's design before the GMU can start** (INFERRED that nothing else can host the image); the earlier E3 could not have started it, and the question of who owns the GPU SMMU now gates E3b (3.1 row 14, stages file E3b).
4. Sizes: the EL2 bring-up L; the monitor adoption M; the kext S to M (build-gated on private headers); the plug-in L (3,498 lines in seven register-writing files); the compiler M (S once Mesa builds); the firewall seam L; the guest tree and display M. The first Metal image also needs the non-GPU workstreams (installed macOS and the AuxKC load on the SP11; the BaseSystem never loads third-party kexts: [GUI-BOOT-ROADMAP.md](GUI-BOOT-ROADMAP.md) section 6).

## 8. Risks

**8.1 Licensing.** Linux (GPL-2.0-only) data feeds: the 48-word CP_PROTECT table (isolated, included by nothing), the RSC sequencer and PDC words (firmware-adjacent), **the `a740_hwcg` table (54 pairs), the `hw_init` constants and the reglists**, and the HFI and GMU sequences; the plug-in's port of Mesa is MIT, which is fine. Rules: reimplement independently, keep generated tables in GPL-labelled files and never paste Linux code into MIT files. Open decisions: (i) the a7xx table, and now the HWCG and `hw_init` data, in a monitor, payload or kext build make that binary a combined GPL work (the notices already say so for the X2 table); (ii) the RSC words: read them back first (E2) and write only if absent; (iii) the provenance text noted in 3.5. The firmware blobs and the Denali image stay local; `gen70500_*` are redistributable under linux-firmware's terms but are not committed here. **Rule applied to the documents of this plan** (pending B2): the plan, the stages file and the marks in v1 and the study give the order, the register names, the guard logic and the line citations of Linux's `hw_init`, GMU boot and RSC and PDC sequences and none of their written values; a table of those values is GPL-sourced data and is held outside this tree until the decision. Single facts with a cite (an offset, a mask, an observed word, the chip id, the GBIF table value, PLL1's L and alpha, which are the 440 MHz rate in the PLL's own formula) are kept.

**8.2 PAC and ABI.** Change constants and add a build profile; keep `AdrenoAcceleratorX2`, `snapintosh,adreno-x2` and every virtual; any new override or class is a panic risk (the KDK recipe on the A16 dev Mac, W23). The monitor's guest ABI must not change.

**8.3 Thermal.** The public trips are SoC-sensor values (passive 95 C, critical 115 C; `hamoa.dtsi:9664-9690` for zone 0, the other seven identical); a tablet's chassis limit is lower and the fan status is in no file read (decision B1). Boot 29: 38 to 43 C at idle (OBSERVED: the raw words; the tenths of a degree INFERRED). First loads at 300 MHz with a wall-clock bound, thermal reads before and after every stage, an abort at the limit of B1, and no ceiling raise without a separate authorisation. ACD is skipped (3.1 row 15), so stay at the low OPPs. The A16 guard samples the A16's sensors: N9 maps tsens3 5 to 12.

**8.4 The abort guard that does not catch an SError.** `ops.load32` is a recoverable-fault guard for synchronous aborts (error 6); it cannot catch an SError and **cannot stop a bus hang** (the q1n1 fork's `SP11-PAYLOAD.md`, ABI section). The runner keeps `PSTATE.A` set (SError masked) and has its own poll (`Q1N1 platform/uefi/main.c:771-825`), which is used only by the proxy and the `smmumap` step (`main.c:1535-1548`); no payload op exposes it (`puts`, `hex`, `load32`, `counter`), so after a masked SError a writing payload would carry on and write (INFERRED for the generic chain). Reads behind a gate must follow a *proof* of the gate; no write may follow an unproved read; hangs end in a power cycle with no log and unknown DRAM retention. Mitigations: N2, write-ahead `NEXT` lines, restore-first abort paths and short chains.

**8.5 The 1 MiB `CP_MEMCPY` rule.** A 4 MiB copy hung the A16 GPU (the cause is unknown); AGENTS.md non-negotiable 5 caps any copy at 1 MiB. The a7xx part has a different UCHE and GMEM (3 MiB): 1 MiB is a ceiling to climb to, not a known-safe size; 4 KiB first, each step clean; refuse anything above 1 MiB and anything not dword-aligned.

**8.6 GMU memory and SMMU.** The GMU DMAs through stream 5 into an arena that EL2 builds: a wrong mapping faults the GMU, and an overlap or an unclean cache state corrupts firmware code; the SMMU is shared with the GPU's own streams (0 and 1) and, per the IORT, streams 2, 4 and 7. Do not rewrite tables under a running GMU (the A16 rule, `gmu_state`). Others: TZ and SCM from EL2, and the RPMh and SMMU owner under QHEE or Gunyah, are UNKNOWN (v1 R2).
