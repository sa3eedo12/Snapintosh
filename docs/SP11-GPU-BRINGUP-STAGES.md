# SP11 GPU bring-up stages E1 to E7 and the data behind them (the hardware half of the version 3 port plan)

This is the hardware half of [SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN-V3.md](SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN-V3.md) (the plan, called "the plan" below; its sections, labels, pins and keys apply here). It replaces the stage ladder S0 to S7 of [SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN.md](SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN.md) section 7 for gates X8a and X8b with E1 to E7, keeps v1's safety rules, and holds the data that the stages need: the order of Linux's a7xx `hw_init` for the X1-85 by register name and line citation, without its written values (section 4), the layout of the public GMU image (section 5) and the restore rules of E2 and E3a (section 6). Each stage needs its own record, payload SHA-256 and approval; none is authorised here.

Labels. VERIFIED: read in the cited pinned public text, or shown by a host test that ran; never a statement about the unit. OBSERVED: boots 29 to 31 (one unit, one boot each) or the unit's own firmware tables read offline (the firmware's declaration, not a read of the hardware). INFERRED, EXPERIMENTAL, UNIMPLEMENTED and UNKNOWN as in the plan. The numbered unknowns U1 to U24 and the decision ids B1 to B7 are the plan's (sections 9 and 10 there). The data in sections 4 to 6 are facts read at the pin and cited by line, not Linux's text. **This file holds no written value of Linux's `hw_init`, GMU boot or RSC and PDC sequences:** the values are at the cited lines of the pinned Linux file, and a machine-readable table of them is GPL-sourced data that is held outside this tree until the licensing decision (plan section 8.1, decision B2); it belongs in a GPL-labelled file, never in an MIT one. Single facts with a cite (an offset, a mask, an observed word, the chip id, the GBIF table value) are kept.

Pins. `LNX` = torvalds/linux `7475ea8b58616b33c04afb976cf0fbaad9dd63fb`, `A/` = `drivers/gpu/drm/msm/adreno/`, `CLK/` = `drivers/clk/qcom/`, `DT` = `arch/arm64/boot/dts/qcom/`; `MESA` = mesa `bbeb2dca9feed6ac88d71234a9dc5ae8aeed4275`; `Q1N1` = the q1n1 fork read at `3d1c7ba`. Effort sizes: S under a day, M a few days, L a week or more.

## 1. Common pre-conditions and rules

AC power, battery at least 50 %, no other hardware client, a thermal baseline printed (tsens3 sensors 5 to 12; **refuse to write if any reads above the threshold of decision B1**: the proposal is 45 C to start and an abort at 60 C), a payload built from tracked sources, its audit and mutants passing, an independent review and the film recorded from start to end. Every stage prints `NEXT <block>` **before** the access, so that the last line names the access that hung. Chain only payloads that are approved together. **Every stage restores what it changed**, but a restore must not poll what Linux does not poll: a CBCR halt poll is bounded at 200 x 1 us (`CLK/clk-branch.c:80`); disabling a *voted* or delay branch is only `udelay(10)` (75-76); disabling a VOTABLE GDSC is `SW_COLLAPSE` plus `udelay(500)` with **no status poll** (`CLK/gdsc.c:166-175`) (section 6).

## 2. The stages

**E1: GPUSNAP2, the read-only complement (no GPU power).** Purpose: decide whether S1 needs rail votes, and baseline the thermal, speed-bin and clock state. Reads: the command database in DRAM (`0x81c60000` and `0x20000` bytes, `DT hamoa.dtsi:516-520`): the addresses of `cx.lvl`, `mx.lvl`, `gfx.lvl`, **`gmxc.lvl`, `gbx.lvl`** and the BCMs `SH0`, `MC0` and `ACV`; the APPS RSC drv-2 only (`0x17520000`, tcs-offset `0xd00`, 3 active, 2 sleep and 2 wake TCS, `hamoa.dtsi:8599-8613`; live levels show only in the wake TCS); the GCC GPLL0 vote words (`0x52030`, `0x52000`: CPH and DIV bits); the GPUCC PLL0 and PLL1 mode, opmode, L and alpha (`0x0` and `0x1000`), the GMU and hub RCG words and **`GPU_CC_CB_CLK` `0x93a4`**; tsens3 sensors 5 to 12 (fix the boot 29 decode first); the qfprom bin cell at `0x221c8000` + `0x119`. Writes: none. Risk: low to medium (new windows: the RSC and the qfprom; RSC drv-0 and drv-1 are never touched). Abort: the first `load32` error ends that window; no retries. An SError is not seen by the payload unless N2 (plan section 4) is in, so E1 also proves N2's poll harmless.

**E2: GPUCX, the S1 power-on, the R5 reads and the collapse.** Purpose: can EL2 switch CX on at the rails' current levels, and read what boot 30 could not. Assert before writing: the CX word has HW_CONTROL and SW_OVERRIDE clear, waits 2, 2 and 0xf and RETAIN_FF already set (boot 30: `0x22F801`, OBSERVED); the hub and GMU RCGs are on XO (source 0). Writes (about 20 stores, all restored): GPUCC CX_GDSCR bit 0 clear, then the RETAIN_FF read-modify-write (a no-op if already set); CBCR enables in DT order for the seven GMU-node clocks and the SMMU's four, and **`0x93a4`** (Linux enables it at probe; the read-back is recorded); the reverse order at the end, SW_COLLAPSE last (no poll). No RPMh, no PLL, no GX and no GMU start. Reads once CX is up: the CX_MISC TCM retention, the GMU init and fence words, the RSC sequencer and TCS state (programmed by the firmware or not), and **the full SMMU state that E3b depends on**: ID0 to ID2 and ID7, sCR0, sGFSR, **every SMR and S2CR**, CBAR and CBA2R, and SCTLR, TTBR0, TCR and MAIR of the banks (the A16 template reads "every SMR and S2CR"): how stream 5 and the banks are left, and whether the hypervisor-style ownership is visible (U4). Risk: medium (a GDSC on under a bad parent clock can wedge; reads behind CX may SError or hang: a power cycle and no log). Abort: a GDSC status poll beyond 2 ms (`gdsc.c:50`), a CBCR halt beyond 200 us, any `load32` error, any SError flagged by N2 or any gate word that differs from boot 30: restore and stop. End with the boot 30 words re-read (expect `DRIFT NONE`).

**E3a: GMUCLK, Linux's clock set for the GMU, and nothing else.** Purpose: can EL2 bring up the clocks the GMU runs on in Linux. Writes (GCC and GPUCC only): the GPLL0 vote (`0x52030` bit 0), the CPH vote (`0x52000` bit 15), the PLL1 configuration from `gpu_cc_pll1_config`, then enable and wait for lock, the GMU RCG source PLL1 /2 (the 220 MHz entry), the hub RCG GPLL0 /3 (200 MHz), then the E2 clock enables; the restore is the reverse (the RCGs back to XO first). Abort: any lock or RCG-update poll timeout (use the A16 `_pll_on` rule: never leave a PLL running unlocked) and restore. Risk: medium (PLL lock; a wedged RCG). Whether the GMU may instead run on XO, which is not Linux's configuration, is a question for E3c and UNKNOWN until then (U19).

**E3b: GMUMAP, the GMU's address space (mandatory before E3c).** Purpose: build what `gen70500_gmu.bin` needs: an EL2-reserved DRAM arena (Linux's layout, plan section 3.1 row 14, sized conservatively because what the firmware uses beyond its 80,532 B is UNKNOWN), LPAE tables, **an SMMU context and SMR and S2CR entries for stream 5** (template `cmd_gmu_smmu` of `Q1N1 tools/a16gpu_stage3.py`: USFCFG set so that unmatched streams fault, read-back verification), and the four firmware blocks copied to their targets (the data file with its SHA-256 and size recorded; the 80,532 B block into the arena at the offset of IOVA `0x4000`, the rest later through the TCM at E3c), with cache maintenance for a non-coherent GMU (INFERRED). The GMU stays in reset. Verify by reading the bank back and, if ID0 reports address translation operations, by translating the mapped IOVAs. Writes: the DRAM arena and the SMMU's global and context-bank registers (**a write to the firmware's SMMU configuration: a new class of write, decision B7**); no GMU register. It depends on **who owns the GPU SMMU** (U4): E2's SMR, S2CR and CB read decides whether a context bank for stream 5 can be programmed and which one; if the unit's firmware or a hypervisor holds it, E3b ends with a refusal, which is itself the datum. Abort: any SMMU read-back mismatch: undo (clear the S2CR and SMR, disable the bank) and stop; never start the GMU. Risk: medium to high (an SMMU misconfiguration can fault other masters that share it; a hypervisor-owned bank can reset the board: [SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN.md](SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN.md) section 7.2).

**E3c: GMUGO, the GMU cold start up to the init result only (a skeleton; designed after the E3a and E3b films).** Purpose: the first execution of `gen70500_gmu.bin`, the init result and the chip-id handshake (`0x7050001`); GX is never requested. Writes, in Linux's order (the written values are at the lines cited in plan section 3.1 row 5, not here): the FAL veto; TCM retention; (the RSC handshake only after a prior stop); the firmware blocks, 476 B to ITCM and 40 B and 24 B to DTCM (dword offsets from the block address); the init-result clear; the boot config; the HFI table address and info and the log word; the fence range; `CM3_CFG`; the chip-id word (`0x7050001`); the **CX GBIF table (4 x `0x00071620`) through the GPU window**; **`GMU_SYS_BUS_CONFIG`, `ICACHE_CONFIG` and `DCACHE_CONFIG` written and `GMU_GENERAL_9` cleared while reset is held**; the `CM3_SYSRESET` release; and the RSC and PDC words of plan section 3.1 row 4 **only if E2 shows them unprogrammed**. Skipped on purpose and recorded as deviations: ACD, the DDR vote and the IRQ masks. Risk: high (unknown firmware on a GX-off GPU; it may vote rails). Abort: an init result that fails Linux's rule (the DTCM version word, **dword offset `0xff8`, which is byte `+0x3fe0`**, selects `0xbabeface` for `val <= 0x20010004` and otherwise `(val & 0x1ff) == 0x100`; a 100 us step and a 100 ms bound: `A/a6xx_gmu.c:311-336`) leads to a stop in Linux's shutdown order as far as it applies: the fence and WRITEDROPPED clear, the `a6xx_gmu_rpmh_off` TCS poll, `CM3_SYSRESET` set, then `a6xx_rpmh_stop` (`a6xx_gmu.c:1099-1135,1150-1185,658-673`). A GMU that never saw HFI cannot be sent `PREP_SLUMBER` (594), so whether it halts without leaving votes is UNKNOWN (U23). Never continue to HFI after a failure.

**Later stages (each with its own record).** **E4:** HFI in Linux's **non-legacy** order (the performance table, the bandwidth table, ACD skipped, CLX, IFPC only if enabled, THINMEM, CORE_FW_START, START), the DDR bandwidth vote (N13; the lowest OPP, a deviation from Linux's highest) and the lowest-OPP vote (300, 390 and 550 MHz are valid for every bin: `opp-supported-hw 0x1f`, `hamoa.dtsi:4152,4160,4168`). **E5:** an OOB GPU_SET hold of seconds, with thermal reads before and after. **E6:** `hw_init` in Linux's order (section 4), the SQE, the reglist **after** `hw_init`, `CP_ME_INIT`, the protect and SECVID read-backs, NOP, WFI and a fence. **E7:** the copy ladder 4 KiB, 64 KiB, 256 KiB and 1 MiB, each clean (RBBM and CP error registers) before the next, and one deliberate recovery drill; never a copy above 1 MiB (plan section 8.5).

## 3. How the stages relate to the stages of the first version

[SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN.md](SP11-GPU-PORT-PLAN.md) section 7 names the stages S0 to S7 and the read order R0 to R6. S0 is boot 30 (its section 7.1.1; unchanged). E2 holds S1, the read-back of S2 (the RSC and PDC state) and the R5 reads (the read-only words behind the CX gate). The GMU image and clock work that v1 listed as S3 is split into E3a, E3b and E3c, because the clocks of the GMU and its own address space are prerequisites that v1 did not name. HFI and the first vote are E4, the GX power-up by the GMU's OOB request is E5, the ring steps are E6 and E7. The safety sections 7.2 to 7.4 of v1 (hazards, bounded submits, the DVFS ceiling and thermal guard) stay valid; its authorisation table 7.5 and its list of what the X8a model lacks (7.7) are superseded by this document and by plan section 3.1.

## 5. `gen70500_gmu.bin` layout (VERIFIED: a header walk of the public blob, run on the host; derived facts only)

The file is the public `gen70500_gmu.bin`, 81,312 B, SHA-256 `5dfba247d548cabcb892ffa716e8dc82a345fd54b5dbae46ba523230e7ae37dd` (linux-firmware `a1ebb5ad`, identical to `69e51bb2` by the fact-check and again for this document; WHENCE 4.03.11). It is never committed. Each entry is 16 bytes (`addr`, `size`, `type`, `value`) followed by `size` bytes (Linux: `A/a6xx_gmu.c:858-931`; entries with `size == 0` are skipped).

| # | addr | size | type | value | Linux target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 11 | `0x0`, `0x4000`, `0x200`, `0x100000`, `0x200000`, `0x300000`, `0x10004000`, `0x10007000`, `0x10007fdc`, `0x60000000`, `0x60001000` | 0 | 1 (entries 1 to 9), 7 (10 and 11) | `0x1dc`, `0x13a94`, `0x393c`, `0x1aa98`, `0x12c80`, `0x24bf8`, `0x2920`, `0xf84`, `0x18`, `0x1000`, `0x1000` | skipped (INFERRED: region descriptors) |
| 12 | `0x0` | 476 | data | | ITCM (`[0, 16K)`), `GMU_CM3_ITCM_START` + the dword offset |
| 13 | `0x4000` | **80,532** | data | | the icache buffer (DRAM, IOVA `0x4000`, `SZ_16M - SZ_16K` long) |
| 14 | `0x10007f98` | 40 | data | | DTCM (`[0x10004000, +16K)`), the dword offset `(addr - 0x10004000) >> 2` |
| 15 | `0x10007fdc` | 24 | data | | DTCM |

The data is 81,072 B and the 15 headers are 240 B: 81,312 B. The `type` and `value` fields of the four data blocks both hold the word `0xefbeadde` (the bytes `de ad be ef`; read in the walk that was run again for this document). The value `0x13a94` of descriptor 2 equals block 13's size. The descriptors that name `0x100000`, `0x200000` and `0x300000` fall inside the icache range that Linux allocates (`0x4000` to `0x1000000`) but carry no data. The init-result version word is dword offset `0xff8` from `GMU_CM3_DTCM_START`, that is byte `+0x3fe0` of the DTCM (`gmu_read` takes dword offsets: `A/a6xx_gmu.h:139-150`), GMU address `0x10007fe0`, which lies **inside block 15** (`0x10007fdc` to `0x10007ff3`): that is why the two small DTCM blocks matter to E3c's abort rule.

## 6. Restore rules for E2 and E3a (VERIFIED, read)

| Action | Linux behaviour | Source | Plan rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enable a branch clock | the enable bit, then a halt poll of up to 200 x 1 us | `CLK/clk-branch.c:80` | poll at most 200 us, then restore and stop |
| Disable a *voted* branch or `BRANCH_HALT_DELAY` | `udelay(10)`, no poll | `clk-branch.c:75-76` | do not poll |
| Power on the CX GDSC (VOTABLE, status word `0x953c`) | clear SW_COLLAPSE, `udelay(1)`, poll bit 31 for up to 2000 us, `RETAIN_FF` | `CLK/gdsc.c:50,104-125,147-200,285-326` | poll 2 ms |
| Power off a VOTABLE GDSC | set SW_COLLAPSE, `udelay(500)`, **no status poll** | `gdsc.c:166-175` | record the read-back and never wait for OFF (another vote may hold it) |
| `gdsc_init` | clear HW_CONTROL and SW_OVERRIDE, write the waits 2, 2 and 0xf | `gdsc.c:436-456` | assert, do not assume (boot 30's `0x22F801` already holds them and RETAIN_FF) |
| Never leave a PLL running unlocked | (the A16 `_pll_on` rule) | `Q1N1 tools/a16gpu_stage3.py` | PLL1 off if the lock fails |
